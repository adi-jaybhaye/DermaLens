### 1. Brand Concept & Design Philosophy

The **DermaLens** identity unites clinical dermatology rigor with botanical vitality and modern optical analysis. Based on **Option 1 — Monogram Elegance**, the mark is built on three core pillars:

1. **The Letter "D" (Dermatology / Derma):**
   - A statuesque, high-contrast Didone serif letterform.
   - Left vertical column in **Soft Blush / Terracotta** (`#D6B4AA` &rarr; `#C47A65`) with bracketed serifs.
   - Sweeping grand arch in **Deep Mocha** (`#3A332F`) arching smoothly to the golden-ratio apex and returning cleanly into the baseline.

2. **The Letter "L" (Lens / Light / Life):**
   - An interlocking vertical serif stem positioned within the counter of the "D".
   - Bilateral top serif in Deep Mocha (`#3A332F`), transitioning into the **Terracotta** lower section.
   - An extended horizontal arm sweeping rightward along the baseline past the outer "D" bowl and terminating with a regal upward-curved serif terminal.

3. **The Botanical Leaf (Natural Skincare & Optical Aperture):**
   - A single, slender lanceolate leaf in **Muted Sage Green** (`#8E9E8F` with `#A5B8A6` highlight and `#768777` depth).
   - Sprouts from the base of the "L" stem on a delicate mocha tendril, arching diagonally at ~40&deg;.
   - Features a crisp, delicate central vein (`#F9F6F1` Ivory hairline) splitting the leaf into two illuminated facets.
   - Bridges the geometric stability of the Roman letterforms with organic vitality, symbolizing skin renewal, health, and visual focus.

### 4. Typography System

- **Primary Wordmark ("Derma"):**  
  High-contrast Didone/Transitional Serif (`Cormorant Garamond` / `Playfair Display`), Weight 700, Deep Mocha (`#3A332F`), Letter-spacing: `-0.5px`.
- **Secondary Wordmark ("Lens"):**  
  Matching Serif weight 700, Fresh Terracotta gradient (`#D9B7AD` &rarr; `#C87B66`), Letter-spacing: `-0.5px`.
- **Tagline ("SEE SKIN DIFFERENTLY"):**  
  Geometric Modern Sans (`Outfit` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`), Weight 600, Uppercase, Tracking `5.5px` (`0.32em`), Deep Mocha (`#3A332F` at 88% opacity).
